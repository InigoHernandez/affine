Start here

AFFINE turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a jungle break chopper: Amens on the keys, an effect under every key, and a PS1-era sound chip after it all.

On the FM-1

Tap SEL: the screen lists what SEL does with the other buttons. The first start offers a tutorial of 28 steps; GLO › SETUP's K3 starts it again.

Install

From the installer, over USB, in Chrome or Edge. Or try it in the browser first.

  1. Back up what your FM-1 holds, with the firmware it runs now. Installing another firmware is at your own risk.
  2. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge and connect the FM-1 by USB, straight to the computer.
  3. Press Install. The FM-1 restarts into AFFINE. Coming from another firmware, leave it running as it is.

What it keeps

Its settings and 64 patterns, in the FM-1's data area: the places LOAF BREAKER and CHOPPAH use. It doesn't read what another firmware saved there: it starts fresh. Its first SAVE writes over that area, so back up first.

Leave it

Install another firmware as usual, from its installer or The Firmware Hub: AFFINE hands over to it. The installer's Back to the stock firmware puts M-VAVE's back.

If it won't start

After two failed starts it comes up in SAFE MODE, silent, with USB on: install it again from the installer. Or hold OCT− while switching the FM-1 on, then install.

Find your way

Four rules, the same on every page.

  1. The encoders never change. SELECT the tempo, ALGORITHM the break, PRESETS the pattern.
  2. A page button opens its first part. Pressed again, the next part, round. It acts as you let go.
  3. OCT− and OCT+ step through the part's items. Where a part has none, they change the pitch.
  4. K1–K4 are the item's. Their names are at the screen's foot.

Play the break

Ten of Cyberworm's royalty-free Amens, sixteen slices each on the white keys.

White keysThe slices. Held, K1–K4 set that slice: LEN, PITCH, START, MODE. With SEL, back to its defaults
ALGORITHMThe next break, or the one before
SELECTThe tempo
OCT− OCT+The pitch, on HOME and EDIT › BREAK

HOME › SETTINGS

HOME again. K1 VIEW, K2 GATE, K3 WAVE ZOOM, K4 BREAK TIME.

EDIT › BREAK

K1 PITCH (KEEP LEN: each slice keeps its length; SPEED: as a tape), K2 GRAIN, K3 SNAP, K4 LEVEL.

The chip

After the chain, the sound of a 1990s console's chip: CODEC, VERB, CRUSH, REDUX, SKIP, then OUT. FX four times: PSX.

Item (OCT)K1–K4
PRESETK1: SUBTLE GLUE, POLY PAD, CRUNCHY TAIL, DUB SPACE (every stage at once)
VERBTYPE, SIZE, TONE, DEPTH. Nine types, from ROOM to DELAY; on CHAOS and DELAY, SIZE steps in note values
CODECMODE (GRIT, the console's sample sound; XA, its CD audio), RATE, BITS, MIX
COLORAMOUNT, RATE, CRUNCH, SMOOTH
LOFIHow CRUSH and REDUX break the sound: CRUSH, MASK, REDUX, JITTER (their amounts are HOME's K2 and K3)
SKIPLENGTH, CHANCE, SEEK, MIX: a disc that skips, on the tempo
OUTCONSOLE, MONO, SEND, INPUT

Save, undo, INIT

Keep it, take it back, or start from the bare break.

SAVEWrites it all to the FM-1. Best while stopped
AUTO SAVEGLO › SETUP's K4: a minute after a change, once stopped
SEL + SAVEUndo; again, redo
INITSEL + HOME, then HOME within 3 s: the pattern cleared and every effect off. The tempo, the break and the sounds stay. SEL + SAVE brings it all back
RESETGLO › RESET: K4 picks what (a drop, the drums, the mix… EVERYTHING), K2 turned five times, then GLO
