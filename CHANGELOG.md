# Changelog

What changed for whoever plays AFFINE, newest first.

## 2026-11-16

- **Site:** the cards answer as polygons and degrade digitally, each its own way (TEAR, GRIT, SLICE, WASH: a
  glitch burst, then washed out and scanned), the words always readable.
- **Screen:** every text on the FM-1 at 2x at least (the owner: "everything in the UI looks very small"), every
  page laid out again for it: pictures in place of help prose (EFFECTS, MIX, SYSTEM, LIVE SETTINGS, BOOM and the
  drums, OVERLAY, SONG, PATTERN, RESET, the tutorial, the SEL card), the tabs as the open one and a square for each,
  the foot's knob names in short forms, the header in one or two lines; no text cut or on another in any of the
  simulator's 100 sessions.
- **Site:** the FM-1's screen and the panel beside it one height in The chip and Perform; the waveform takes the
  room left.
- **Site:** the header's gem is HOME's, in 3D: a pointer on it throws it into a spin.
- **Site:** no Skins section (the header's switch does it); the black keys' names fit their pills, in one row or
  two; no text past its box or the screen at any width (the numbers a size down in narrow tiles, shorter titles);
  the knobs' gauges level; the installer's title on one line.
- **Screen:** HOME draws in a quarter of the time (the same picture, pixel for pixel), and comes later while the
  sound is busy, so the FM-1 shows the performance effects instead of falling behind; LOAF's header no longer
  flashes over HOME on every beat.
- **Site:** the background grid closed on the right, its last column ruled like the others.
- **Release 0.1:** the first public build, a beta not yet run on an FM-1: the installer on the site
  (<https://inigohernandez.github.io/affine/install/>) and the GitHub release v0.1 carry the same file. The site's
  FM-1 runs 0.1 too.
- **Site:** AFFINE's page, <https://inigohernandez.github.io/affine/>: the firmware running in the browser, the
  chip's five stages to hear, the black keys to hold, the five skins.
- **Screen:** AFFINE's logo (speed stripes into chrome letters) and a 3D splash at power-on.
- **Screen:** HOME in one-point perspective: sixteen even lanes, the gem turning with the break, every performance
  effect drawn on the scene.
- **Safety:** a store from another firmware is left alone; under heavy load the chip's stages step back (CODEC,
  then SKIP, then VERB) instead of glitching; three memory bugs fixed.

## 2026-11-15

- **Fork:** AFFINE begins from LOAF BREAKER: the hardcore kick out, BOOM in (808, JUNGLE, DUB, TIGHT).
- **Sound:** the PS1-era chip after the break: CODEC (GRIT, XA), VERB (nine presets), CRUSH, REDUX, SKIP.
- **Perform:** HOME's knobs FILTER, CRUSH, REDUX, VERB; the black keys SKIP, VERB THROW and CRUSH; the PSX page.
- **Screen:** every page in POLYGON and the pixel font; five skins.
- **Breaks:** Cyberworm's royalty-free Amens.
